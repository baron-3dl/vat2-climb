02 the idea3 / 11

Separate state from compute. Let selection find the architecture.

Bodies grow from a grammar in a genome under 1 KB. The genome writes the learning rule too. No backward pass, so a body's weights are its only state: it lives on any card and moves mid-life.

A reaper keeps the climbers. A separate process, the stoker, appends rungs. Wasting the card is fatal.

06 the ramp7 / 11

The curriculum has no top. It ends at a conversation.

Reading span grows from one glyph to words to sentences from public-domain readers and on up the corpus bands. Recall grows with vocabulary. The stoker appends a rung whenever bodies saturate the last one.

Destination: a microphone, "hello", and an answer that generalises. The reflex that maps one sound to another is easy and excluded.

07 scale8 / 11

Three multipliers. None waits on the others.

×N
workers
Any card, any owner: lease a slice, tick, publish, release. A dead worker loses nothing. Contributors run the same binary; trust is a replication factor on selection-critical reads only.
×1000
body size
27B weights is three grammar depth steps from today's founder. Int8 residency, no duplicate copies, paging, cross-card partition; plasticity that cools with age keeps learning cost on the growth front.
∞
rungs
The stoker climbs the corpus bands on its own clock. Its ceiling is how fast text is staged: a people-and-scripts problem, not GPUs.

Compute honesty: today's tick launches small kernels per body and block. At 27B that must become a few fused, bandwidth-bound kernels per card, about 30 ms per tick. Filed as an engineering front; it is what the rack is for.
