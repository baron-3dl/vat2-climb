What it is doing

A body sees a blurred glyph and has to draw the sharp one back.

Every rung is the same task with the blur turned down. No one hands the body a score: it is graded on its own marks, it watches a demonstration, and its plasticity, written into its genome, does the rest within a single life.

The exam

Reproduce what you perceive. The difficulty knob is the blur sigma, from 1.25 down to zero in ten rungs. The grade is the overlap between the body's own marks and the sharp glyph, and the body sees its own marks, so it can learn from the mismatch.

The body

A recurrent net of 64-unit blocks, grown from a sub-kilobyte genome by a grammar. The genome also writes the learning rule, a small polynomial over what each synapse can see. Forward passes only; nothing is backpropagated, so a body can live on any card and be moved mid-life.

The population

One reaper reads every body's position on the ladder and keeps the ones that climb. Replacements are bred by mutation and recombination, with lineage tags so we can see which kind of founder wins. Size is limited by the card, not by a parameter: wasting the GPU is fatal.

Rung reached, every round of both runs

What it has not shown yet, stated plainly: the acuity ladder tops out at the sharp glyph, so both runs ended parked on rung 10. The next universe carries axes with no top: reading span that grows into corpus text, and recall that grows with vocabulary, plus a curriculum process that appends rungs as bodies saturate. The reads that would settle abstraction versus memorisation are not interpretable in these two runs, and we say so in the pre-registration.

Join the boiler

Any machine with a card is a worker. Point it at the store.

The universe is a store, not a process. Workers lease a slice of the population, run it for a round, write the results back and release. A worker that dies loses nothing: its lease expires and another picks the lives up from the last published state. The curriculum is written into the same store by a separate process, so the population never runs out of rungs and no batch can cap it.

curl -sO http://<store-host>:8397/grand.tgz && tar xzf grand.tgz
python3 scripts/boiler_worker.py --store http://<store-host>:8777 \
        --device cuda:0 --slice-size 24

Trust is a replication factor, not a promise: selection-critical measurements can be required from more than one independent worker before the reaper believes them. Bulk ticking stays single-copy, because a dishonest worker there only breeds a body that loses.

Why forward-only matters here

Nothing to lose mid-flight.

With no backward pass there is no gradient state to checkpoint or synchronise. A body's weights are its only state, published periodically, so slices can move between cards, machines and contributors while the run continues. The same store that evolves a brain is the store that serves it afterwards: to deploy, point workers at its genome's slice.
